Add the Focus pane to the Tree. Click a ticket and its pane opens right under it with the ticket's state, who claimed it, and its title as a link; click the ⚑ Map row and the pane shows the map's counts and its destination. ↗ Open takes you to the issue on GitHub, or opens the ticket's file on a local tracker. Click the row again or press ✕ to close the pane. The Cockpit now remembers which maps are expanded and which row is selected, so after you reload the window it opens where you left it.
