Show drift in the Cockpit. When a map or a ticket is written in an older or odd form, the Tree now tells you whether what it shows can be trusted. A ⚠ after a ticket's title means something it shows may be wrong or missing (a ticket with no type, or one that waits on names instead of numbers); hover it for the reason. A map shows ⚠ when it or any of its tickets, open or closed, has such a warning, and its tooltip counts them. Old forms that were still read correctly stay quiet: no mark. The Focus pane lists each loud warning on its own line, one muted line for the old forms ("2 old forms") and, on a map, how many tickets drift; click a line to open the Detail at its new Drift section. The Detail ends with that section, every entry giving what was found and a line on the current convention, and a ⚠ chip in its header jumps to it. Dismiss an entry and it stays hidden, also after a reload, until what it found changes. A map whose body is not in the current form shows the sections that could be read, a note, and the body as written; tickets that belong to no map are listed in a folded **Unmapped** node above Finished. Drift never changes the order of anything, and it never raises a badge or a notification. If a warning says a newer mattpocock-skills may write this form, check for a Hero Synergy update.
