Add the Cockpit Detail. Press Enter or double-click a map or ticket row in the Tree, or press ↗ Detail in its pane, and the full issue opens in an editor tab titled `#number title`; the tab follows the Tree's selection from then on. **Hero Synergy: Open Cockpit Detail** opens it on the selected row, or on the first map when nothing is selected. A ticket shows its state, type, claim, the tickets it waits on and the tickets it clears the way for (click one and the Tree selects it and unfolds its map), its body as rendered Markdown and, once closed, its resolution. A map shows its destination, decisions so far, Not yet specified and Out of scope; press Enter on the Fog or Decisions row of a map and its Detail opens scrolled to that section. After you reload the window the Detail reopens on the same selection. The pane's title is still the link to the issue itself, and a link inside a body opens in your browser when it is an `http` or `https` one.
